## Sports lighting — what makes it different
- Low glare: proprietary COB light engine + polished aluminium reflector +
  99%-transmission glass lens with Fresnel pattern (never yellows); optional
  external glare shields in multiple lengths for RT410 and M Series.
- Precision optics: 18° long-throw to 50°/100° short-throw beams, crisp edge
  cut-off, minimal spill and obtrusive light.
- Flicker-free broadcast drive: VSP (Vision Strobe Protection) series uses
  stable high-frequency drive to eliminate flicker in slow-motion HDTV replay —
  the requirement ordinary LED floodlights fail in televised venues.
- Modular architecture: M Series scales from a single 1M module (80W) to 16M
  (1280W+) in one housing, keeping optics and spare parts common across a
  tennis court and a 20,000-seat stadium.
- Performance: 130 lm/W, up to 120,000+ lm per fixture, CRI 70–95,
  CCT 3000–5700K, L70 >100,000 h @ 25°C, 5+ year warranty.
- Outdoor-hardened: IP66, 10kV surge (common + differential mode), −40°C to
  55°C operation, 347–480VAC or 110–277VAC input, Bridgelux LEDs,
  Inventronics drivers, stainless steel 304 or aluminium + glass housings.
- Engineering service: DIALux photometric layouts plus structural design
  delivered with the proposal; full photometric proposal within 48 hours.
- Energy outcome: 50%+ saving versus legacy HID; intelligent control and
  interval-lighting modes add a further ~50% on selected arenas.

## High-ambient, dusty and coastal operation
Ratings as published per model. Nineteen luminaire models carry the full
rating set; accessory and series-overview pages do not.
- Ambient operating range: **−40°C to 55°C**. The 55°C upper limit is the
  figure to specify against in hot-climate venues, where summer ambient
  temperature is the usual reason a luminaire has to be derated.
- Ingress protection: **IP66** (IEC 60529). The first digit — 6 — is the dust
  rating and means the enclosure is dust-tight, which is the relevant
  protection on sandy and dusty sites; the second digit covers powerful water
  jets.
- Surge protection: **10 kV**, common mode and differential mode, with wide
  input 347–480 VAC or 110–277 VAC.
- Housing: aluminium + glass as standard; **stainless steel 304** housings on
  selected models for corrosive or coastal atmospheres.
- Lumen maintenance: **L70 >100,000 h at 25°C** — the standard rating
  temperature. SolarOne does not publish a separate high-ambient
  lumen-maintenance figure.
Salt-spray test reports are held for selected models and supplied on request;
SolarOne publishes no site-wide salt-spray hours figure. SolarOne does not hold
SASO or G-Mark certification and offers no products certified to either mark.
Request the model-specific datasheet before specifying for a coastal or
high-corrosion site.
