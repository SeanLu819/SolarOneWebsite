SolarOne tennis court lighting covers both outdoor and indoor tennis courts. Outdoor tennis court lighting pairs our LED sports floodlights with galvanized light poles for even, low-glare coverage across the full court; indoor tennis court lighting uses the same optics in ceiling- and wall-mounted arrangements to eliminate shadows over the net and baseline. The result: uniform lux levels, broadcast-ready colour, and lower energy use than legacy HID.
